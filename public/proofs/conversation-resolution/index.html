E01Client requirements caseReconcile evolving requirements

Conversation sources became individually identified wants with source references, evolution, scope and implementation status. The record exposed a mismatch between the governing experience and a nested product funnel.

Applied here. Connect the customer statement, its interpretation and the authorized implementation; preserve disagreement.

Private client-delivery evidence. Not proof of automated extraction accuracy or customer-scale throughput; correspondence is not published.

E02Client review studioFocus the human decision

Parallel editorial, archive, commerce and platform lanes feed one client-review item. Preparation is distinct from rights, voice, editorial and publication approval.

Applied here. Present one actionable review while specialist work proceeds behind it. Preserve the existing public platform until cutover is authorized.

Private operating model and prototype. Publication and migration remain human-gated; not autonomous customer operation.

E03AI Chat ExporterPreserve source conversations

The ChatGPT path normalizes a message tree and supports Markdown, HTML, JSON, PNG and text exports, separating provider, request queue and renderers.

Applied here. Preserve structured evidence before summarizing; put source-specific adapters behind a shared contract.

Substantially transformed fork of Pionxzh's project. Other provider paths are scaffolds; compatibility with changing host interfaces is not guaranteed.

E04Private knowledge pipelineTurn chats into typed records

A knowledge atomizer separates messages and code blocks, retains conversation identity, and applies redaction and format-specific chunking strategies.

Applied here. Make retrievable units without losing their source context.

The inspected message pass skips content under 20 characters and uses random unit IDs. A sales adaptation needs short-approval preservation and replay-safe identity; those limitations are not hidden.

E05LingFramePreserve context across levels

The atomization model retains hierarchical relationships between theme, paragraph, sentence, word and letter.

Applied here. Pair a small, inspectable evidence span with the larger context needed to distinguish a request, condition or commitment.

Research framework and heuristic rhetorical analysis, not a validated business-intent classifier.

E06Public Record Data Scraper / UCCNormalize and deliver into CRM

Inspected HubSpot adapters map prospect data to CRM properties, submit authenticated requests and expose success, failure and remote object identifiers.

Applied here. Translate a shared record into the destination schema and reconcile its returned object instead of adding an isolated store.

Private integration code, not a live customer test, owner-acceptance system or proof of idempotent remote creation.

E07LimenBind execution to authority

Bounded work packets carry authority, limits, deadlines and verification predicates. A deterministic keeper manages leases and generations; receipts record executor identity, revisions, checks and outcomes.

Applied here. Distinguish assignment, acceptance, execution and completion evidence.

An agent lease is not a person's acceptance and does not prove exactly-once effects in an external CRM.

How this would fit a real team

Keep the tools people already use.

A call or message stays linked to its customer record. The next person sees the approved request, the owner, and the next action—not every raw note.

Automate routine transitions, not unresolved decisions.

The walkthrough pauses to explain each step. A production workflow should not make one person manually play every role. Existing rules can handle routine routing; people handle unclear intent, exceptions, and approvals that require judgment.

Prove one handoff before expanding it.

A pilot needs real account matching, permissions, reliable storage, CRM integration, delivery checks, and failure recovery. Measure missed requests and time to accepted ownership. This page does not establish those production capabilities.
