# NYC Literary Field Guide methodology

The Literary Field Guide is a maintained public resource for readers, writers, teachers, artists, editors, translators, filmmakers, and organizers across Staten Island, Brooklyn, Manhattan, Jersey City, and Newark. A separate film type brings genuinely free screenings into the same civic discovery surface without changing literary record identities.

## What earns inclusion

An event must have a reliable public source and clear literary substance. Preference goes to events that offer at least one of the following:

- serious craft or artistic insight;
- access to writers, editors, translators, publishers, or cultural institutions;
- uncommon interdisciplinary value across writing, performance, visual art, technology, or civic life;
- a meaningful free or low-cost entry point;
- direct value to an underserved local community, especially Staten Island;
- reusable educational or community-organizing value.

Promotional listings without a discernible public benefit are excluded. The guide is selective rather than exhaustive. Film listings must have unambiguously free admission; mandatory purchases, paid memberships, and unclear suggested-donation terms do not qualify.

## Record standard

Each record includes the official source, date last verified, place, format, price posture, registration status, themes, an editorial explanation, and a statement of community use. Event organizers remain the authority on schedule, access, and ticket changes.

## Reuse

The guide is available as HTML, JSON, and iCalendar. Editorial data is released under CC BY 4.0; source event descriptions and linked materials remain the property of their publishers.

## Corrections and submissions

Use the repository's Literary Field Guide issue form. Include an official event URL and explain the concrete community value. Submissions are reviewed against the same standard as editor-discovered events.
